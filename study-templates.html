Getting more from study planners

Plan around your energy, not just your timetable. Put demanding subjects in the hours when you concentrate best, keep sessions to 25 to 50 minutes, and leave a day each week with no new material. Pair the weekly planner with the daily one: the weekly page shows balance, the daily page shows exactly what to do next.
