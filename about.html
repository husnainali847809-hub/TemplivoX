About TemplivoX

Free templates, designed to be useful the moment you download them.

Our purpose

TemplivoX is a library of free printable and editable templates for everyday organising. The goal is simple: give people clean, practical documents they can download in a minute and start using straight away, without sign-ups, pop-ups or clutter.

How the templates are made

Each template is built around one job, such as planning a study week, tracking a debt or writing an invoice. Layouts are kept plain so they print clearly on a home printer, and the PDF versions come in both A4 and US Letter. Editable versions are provided as Word documents and, where numbers are involved, as Excel spreadsheets with working formulas.

What you will find here

The library currently has 29 templates in six categories: study, budget, daily planning, business, calendars and personal life. New ones are added over time, and you can suggest a template you would like to see.

Our approach

Templates are general-purpose tools, not professional advice. Please read the disclaimer before relying on any financial, legal or business template.
