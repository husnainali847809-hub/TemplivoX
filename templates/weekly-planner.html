How to use this template

  1. Download the PDF to print, or the editable file to fill in on a computer.
  2. Set your weekly focus, then add fixed appointments to each day before the flexible tasks.
  3. Print at 100 percent scale on A4 or US Letter paper, or save your edited file as a PDF.

Example weekly overview

Add fixed commitments such as classes, work shifts or appointments before filling the remaining day boxes with flexible tasks. Use the weekly focus area for one outcome you want to make progress on, rather than a long list of unrelated goals.

Practical tips

Review the page at the start and end of the week. Move unfinished tasks deliberately instead of copying every item forward automatically.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from the weekly calendar?

The planner has open boxes for tasks and notes. The weekly calendar is a time-based grid for scheduling hours.

Is the Weekly Planner really free?

Yes. You can download and print it for personal, educational or internal business use at no cost. See the Terms of Use for details.

Which paper sizes are available?

The PDF comes in A4 and US Letter. Print at 100 percent or Actual size so nothing is scaled or cropped.
