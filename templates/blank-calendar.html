How to use this template

  1. Download the PDF to print, or the editable file to fill in on a computer.
  2. Write your own title at the top, number the cells from any starting day, and fill in the plan.
  3. Print at 100 percent scale on A4 or US Letter paper, or save your edited file as a PDF.

Example custom calendar layout

Give the grid a title such as 'Content plan', 'Staff rota' or 'Four-week revision cycle'. Decide what each column and row represents, then label the cells yourself so the same blank layout can support different planning systems.

Practical tips

Write a small key or legend near the grid if you use colours or abbreviations. The blank format has no dates, so it can be adapted to a custom period.

Frequently asked questions

How is it different from the monthly calendar?

It has no notes strip, so the cells are larger. Use it for rotas, content plans or four-week cycles.

Is the Blank Calendar really free?

Yes. You can download and print it for personal, educational or internal business use at no cost. See the Terms of Use for details.

Which paper sizes are available?

The PDF comes in A4 and US Letter. Print at 100 percent or Actual size so nothing is scaled or cropped.
