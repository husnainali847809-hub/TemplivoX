How to use this template

  1. Download the PDF to print, or the editable file to fill in on a computer.
  2. Add a book when you start it and write the finish date and rating when you are done.
  3. Print at 100 percent scale on A4 or US Letter paper, or save your edited file as a PDF.

Example reading log

Add a book when you start it, then record the author, start date, finish date and rating when you complete it. Use the notes column for a memorable idea or a short personal reflection.

Practical tips

Choose one rating scale and use it consistently. The spreadsheet is useful for sorting a longer reading list or reviewing books completed during the year.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use it for a reading challenge?

Yes. Count the finished rows to see your progress toward a yearly goal.

Is the Reading Tracker really free?

Yes. You can download and print it for personal, educational or internal business use at no cost. See the Terms of Use for details.

Which paper sizes are available?

The PDF comes in A4 and US Letter. Print at 100 percent or Actual size so nothing is scaled or cropped.
