How to use this template

  1. Download the PDF to print, or the editable file to fill in on a computer.
  2. Fill fixed commitments first, then place flexible tasks in the gaps.
  3. Print at 100 percent scale on A4 or US Letter paper, or save your edited file as a PDF.

Example weekly time-block schedule

Write the week beginning date, then add fixed commitments such as classes, shifts and appointments in the correct day and time cells. The open spaces make it easier to see when a study session, workout or project task can fit.

Practical tips

Add fixed events first and leave travel or preparation time around them. Edit the Word version if your routine starts earlier or finishes later than the printed hours.
