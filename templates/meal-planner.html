Plan breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks for the week. Plan meals first, then write a grocery list from them. It reduces food waste and last-minute takeaways.

How to use this template

  1. Download the PDF to print, or the editable file to fill in on a computer.
  2. Fill dinners first, then lunches, then check what you already have before writing the grocery list.
  3. Print at 100 percent scale on A4 or US Letter paper, or save your edited file as a PDF.

Yes. Use the notes in each cell for portions or dietary needs.
