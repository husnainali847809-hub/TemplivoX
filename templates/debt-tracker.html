How to use this template

  1. Download the PDF to print, or the editable file to fill in on a computer.
  2. List each debt, then update the balance after every payment.
  3. Print at 100 percent scale on A4 or US Letter paper, or save your edited file as a PDF.

Example debt overview

List each account on its own row with the current balance, interest rate, minimum payment and due date. This gives you one place to compare obligations and see which payment dates are approaching.

Practical tips

Update balances after payments and check rates against your latest statements. The tracker organises information; it does not replace advice from a qualified financial professional.

Frequently asked questions

Which debt should I pay first?

Common methods are paying the highest rate first or the smallest balance first. Pick the one you will stick with.

Is the Debt Tracker really free?

Yes. You can download and print it for personal, educational or internal business use at no cost. See the Terms of Use for details.

Which paper sizes are available?

The PDF comes in A4 and US Letter. Print at 100 percent or Actual size so nothing is scaled or cropped.
