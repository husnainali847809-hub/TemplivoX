How to use this template

  1. Download the PDF to print, or the editable file to fill in on a computer.
  2. Write your main goal for the week first, then fill each day with the subject and the topic you will cover.
  3. Print at 100 percent scale on A4 or US Letter paper, or save your edited file as a PDF.

Example weekly study plan

If you have biology, maths and English to revise, assign each subject to a few focused sessions across the week. Put the hardest topic on a day when you have more energy, and leave space for review instead of filling every box.

Practical tips

At the end of the week, mark what you completed and move unfinished topics into the next week's plan. Keep the weekly goal measurable, such as completing two chapters or finishing one past paper.

Frequently asked questions

How many subjects should I schedule per day?

Two or three is realistic for most students. Short, focused sessions on fewer subjects usually work better than a crowded day.
