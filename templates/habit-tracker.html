How to use this template

  1. Download the PDF to print, or the editable file to fill in on a computer.
  2. Write your habits in the left column, then mark an X each day you complete one.
  3. Print at 100 percent scale on A4 or US Letter paper, or save your edited file as a PDF.

Example monthly habit tracker

Choose a small number of repeatable habits, such as reading for ten minutes, stretching or preparing tomorrow's tasks. Write one habit per row and mark the day when you complete it. The month grid lets you see patterns without relying on memory.

Practical tips

Start with three to five habits and track consistency rather than perfection. If you miss a day, resume the next day instead of abandoning the tracker.

Frequently asked questions

How many habits should I track?

Start with three to five. You can always add more once those feel automatic.
