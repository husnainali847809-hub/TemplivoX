How to use this template

  1. Download the PDF to print, or the editable file to fill in on a computer.
  2. Write your own chores on the lines, then tick them off each period.
  3. Print at 100 percent scale on A4 or US Letter paper, or save your edited file as a PDF.

Example household cleaning routine

Put quick repeat jobs in the Daily section, larger recurring jobs in Weekly, and occasional tasks in Monthly. Each household is different, so use the blank lines for the chores that actually apply to your home.

Practical tips

Share tasks fairly if several people use the checklist. Review the routine after a few weeks and remove jobs that are unnecessary or too frequent.

Frequently asked questions
