How to use this template

  1. Download the PDF to print, or the editable file to fill in on a computer.
  2. List up to three goals, then block out study sessions and breaks in the schedule on the left.
  3. Print at 100 percent scale on A4 or US Letter paper, or save your edited file as a PDF.

Example daily revision schedule

A student preparing for an exam could reserve the morning for a difficult subject, add a short break, and use a later block for practice questions. Put the exact topic beside each study session so the schedule tells you what to do, not just when to sit down.

Practical tips

Keep the goals list short and use the review area to note what was difficult. If your classes start earlier or later, edit the time column in the Word file.
