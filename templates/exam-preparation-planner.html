How to use this template

  1. Download the PDF to print, or the editable file to fill in on a computer.
  2. Copy topics from your syllabus, mark the weakest as high priority, and schedule those first.
  3. Print at 100 percent scale on A4 or US Letter paper, or save your edited file as a PDF.

Example exam revision plan

For a science exam, list each syllabus topic in the revision table, mark weaker topics as high priority, and choose a date to revisit each one. Use the past-paper area to record practice attempts and the exam checklist for items you need on the day.

Practical tips

Do not schedule every topic for the final few days. Spread revision across the available weeks and leave time to practise questions under timed conditions.

Frequently asked questions

When should I start planning revision?

Many students start four to six weeks before an exam. Starting earlier simply gives you more time for past papers.

Is the Exam Preparation Planner really free?
