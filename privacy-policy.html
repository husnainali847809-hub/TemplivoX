Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026.

What we collect

TemplivoX does not require an account and does not store personal information in a database. The contact form does not send data to our servers: it opens your own email app, so anything you write is shared only when you choose to send that email. Searching and filtering templates happens in your browser.

Hosting and logs

The website is served by a third-party hosting provider, which may keep technical logs such as IP address, browser type and requested pages for security and reliability. We do not control those logs.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The site does not currently set cookies of its own. If we later add analytics or advertising, for example Google AdSense, those services may use cookies or similar technologies to measure traffic or show ads. This policy will be updated before that happens and will explain the choices available to you.

Downloads

Template files are downloaded directly from this website. We do not track who downloads which file.

Children

The website is general-purpose and not directed at children under 13.

Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data. Because we hold none, there is usually nothing to access or delete, but you can ask us at husnainali847809@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
